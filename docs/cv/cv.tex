  \textbf{University of Trento}, \role{MSc in AI Systems.}\\
  Thesis: \role{What Do Large Multimodal Models Really Know? Unlocking
  Knowledge Through Test-Time Computation in Open-World Image Classification.}\\
  Advisor: \role{Elisa Ricci.}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Built a 256-sample evaluation framework on the OVEN benchmark separating
          coverage from reliability; showed a lenient LLM judge inverts the ranking
          of Qwen3-VL 2B--32B, while a specificity-preserving audit restores it.
    \item Extended the rsbench benchmark suite to Raven's Progressive Matrices with a
          DeepProbLog solver, exposing a reasoning shortcut: 0.91 answer F1 while two
          of three concepts stayed at chance, removed with 1\% concept supervision.
    \item Compared GPT-2 expert neurons against fMRI recordings from nine subjects
          using representational similarity analysis; expert neurons align with brain
          activity better than the dense embeddings containing them (mean \textit{d} = 0.63).
    \item Improved cache-based Test-Time Adaptation with a waiting-list strategy that
          revisits uncertain samples, targeting non-i.i.d.\ streams.
  \end{itemize}}
